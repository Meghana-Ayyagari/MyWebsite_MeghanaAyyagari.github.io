A Little About Me

I’m an enthusiastic and lively person with a love for all things creative, especially poetry, art, dance, and literature. Physics is another big part of my life. It challenges me more often than I’d like to admit, but that’s also what keeps me curious and pushes me to do better.

My Journey So Far

I’ve spent different chapters of my life in different places, each adding something new to my journey.

  • MumbaiGrew up here and completed my schooling until Class 10.
  • HyderabadMoved here for my higher secondary education, then pursued my B.Sc. in Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science at St. Francis College for Women.
  • Andhra Pradesh · NowCurrently pursuing my M.Sc. in Physics at SRM University–AP.

My Love for Nature

Beyond academics, I’m someone who can never quite get enough of nature. I’m fascinated by its beauty, strength, and the little wonders it creates every day. There’s something comforting about the way nature moves through its cycles of creation and destruction, reminding us that life is always changing, growing, and beginning again. I often find myself looking at these things a little philosophically, discovering small lessons about life, happiness, and simply being.

I guess I’m just someone who loves learning, creating, observing, and finding a little magic in the ordinary.
