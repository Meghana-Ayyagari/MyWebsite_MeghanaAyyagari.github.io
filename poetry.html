Poetry

I love how physics helps me understand the world around me, while poetry allows me to feel it a little more deeply. One teaches me to question, explore, and uncover the mysteries of the universe; the other helps me find meaning in the little things, the fleeting moments, and the beauty of simply being alive. Somewhere between equations and verses, I find a balance between understanding life and experiencing it.
